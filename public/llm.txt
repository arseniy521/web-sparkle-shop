# NIUS - Home Nursing & IV Drips in Prague

> Registered nurses who come to your home, hotel or office in Prague: vitamin and hydration drips, injection support, post-surgery nursing and daily care. We speak Czech, English, Russian and Ukrainian.

## Overview

NIUS (Nius Services s.r.o.) is a home nursing service in Prague. A registered nurse visits you at home, at your hotel or at your office with all equipment. Every infusion is prescribed by a doctor after a short health assessment - the consultation is included in the price.

NIUS is not an emergency service. In an emergency call 155 or 112.

## Services

### Vitamin & hydration drips (IV)
Vitamins, minerals and fluids, chosen with the doctor after a short health assessment. A session takes roughly 35-75 minutes.
- Immunity Lite, Immunity Power
- Vitamin & Mineral Classic, Vitamin & Mineral Plus
- CEO Recharge, Metabolic Reset
- Pure Hydrate, Electrolyte Reset, Jet Lag Recovery (rehydration after travel and busy days)
- B-Power Shot (vitamin B12 injection)
- Optional add-on shots (vitamins, minerals) during the same visit

Prices: from 1,650 CZK. Nurse visit, equipment and doctor consultation are included.
Page: https://www.nius.cz/en/iv-drips-prague/
Rehydration page: https://www.nius.cz/en/rehydration-iv-drip-prague/

### Injection support
We administer medication prescribed by your clinic or doctor, following their written instructions - including injection visits for fertility treatment patients.
- IVF injection visit: from 1,900 CZK
- Page: https://www.nius.cz/en/ivf-support-prague/

### Post-surgery nursing
- Wound dressing and dressing changes: from 1,890 CZK
- Hygiene assistance: from 1,690 CZK
- Nurse escort to and from medical appointments: from 2,190 CZK
Suitable for medical tourists and anyone recovering at home or in a hotel.

### Daily care at home
Help with personal hygiene, mobility and daily routines for elderly and disabled clients.
