Frequently asked

Do I need a prescription for IV therapy?

Every infusion is prescribed by a doctor after a short health assessment, and the consultation is included in the price.

How fast can a nurse arrive?

Same-day visits, daily 8:00–21:00. We confirm the arrival time after you book.

What qualifications do your nurses have?

All nurses are registered and certified with hospital-grade training. They carry full medical equipment.

Can I get an IV drip at my hotel?

Yes. We visit hotels across Prague. Just give us the hotel name and room details when you book.

How long does an infusion take?

Most infusions take 35–75 minutes depending on the drip. Your nurse stays the entire time.

Do you support IVF patients?

Yes. We administer medication prescribed by your clinic, following their written instructions. Evening injection visits are available.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, bank transfer, and card payment. EUR accepted at current rate.

Is there a minimum booking?

No minimum. Single visits, bundles, and monthly subscriptions are all available.
