After your IV drip, the nurse provides aftercare instructions and answers questions. Payment via cash, card, or bank transfer. We'll follow up if you have any concerns.

Tip: Follow your nurse's aftercare advice and drink water after your session.

Preparing for Your IV Drip in Prague

Before Your Session

  • •Eat a light meal 1–2 hours before
  • •Drink water to stay hydrated
  • •Wear comfortable clothing with loose sleeves
  • •Have your medication list ready (if applicable)
  • •Ensure nurse can access your location easily

After Your Session

  • •Rest for 15–30 minutes before activities
  • •Drink water as advised by your nurse
  • •Follow the nurse's aftercare advice
  • •Light exercise is fine; avoid intense workouts same day
  • •Contact us if you have any concerns or questions

When an IV Drip May Not Be Suitable

IV drips may not be suitable if you have:

  • •Severe kidney or heart disease (without medical approval)
  • •Known allergies to IV components
  • •Uncontrolled high blood pressure
  • •Active cancer treatment (consult your oncologist first)

Important: Always disclose your full medical history during screening. Our nurses will assess suitability before proceeding.
